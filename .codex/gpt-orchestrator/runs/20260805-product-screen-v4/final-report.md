# Taobao product-screen V4 visual run

## Outcome

Six accepted 1024×1024 product-screen candidates were produced: Gemini and ChatGPT each supplied one QR-lottery, one imported-list-lottery, and one big-screen interactive-game direction. The selected set is collected under `marketing/taobao-mainimage-v4-product-screen-20260805/source/`, with a 3×2 comparison sheet under `deliverables/`.

## Models and provenance

- Gemini web: primary candidates generated while the page visibly showed Gemini Pro.
- Gemini correction fallback: one optional attempt to remove small game-ranking numerals displayed a peak-load notice and switched to Flash Extended. No accepted output came from this fallback; the three numerals were masked deterministically in the local accepted copy.
- ChatGPT web: page visibly showed 5.6 Sol with high reasoning. Luna was not used.
- Context transport: sanitized public/product-screen references only. No QianNiu, SYCM, order, customer, account, or private operating data was sent.

## Visual verification

- All six accepted images are square 1024×1024 PNG files.
- The imagery is limited to buyer-visible front-end big-screen effects.
- No admin dashboard, operation flow, phone, presenter, crowd, venue, physical monitor, readable selling copy, logo, QR code, price, watermark, or unsupported claim appears in the accepted set.
- Abstract placeholder lines and generic icons are intentionally retained for later deterministic Chinese typography.

## Editorial recommendation

- QR lottery: Gemini is closer to the current product's participant-wall morphology; ChatGPT is cleaner and more premium but slightly more stylized.
- Imported-list lottery: ChatGPT has the clearest rolling-name-slot hierarchy; Gemini feels more like an established event-screen product.
- Interactive game: Gemini is closer to the recognizable car-race product; ChatGPT has stronger polish but a more stylized neon race form.

## External writes

Only provider conversations and local visual artifacts were created. No QianNiu product, listing, publishing, or advertising configuration was changed.
