Style/medium: high-end editorial 3D advertising render with subtle premium glass materials; clean, elegant, commercially polished, realistic stage lighting; not a crowded cyberpunk composition.

Composition/framing: exact square 1:1 composition. Color palette: deep navy, ivory white, a small amount of mint cyan only.

Materials/textures: subtle glass and soft light, refined stage surfaces, no excessive holographic UI layers.

Text: none.

Constraints: every list card must be an abstract blank bar/card with absolutely no markings; no readable text, no Chinese characters, no letters, no numbers, no names, no symbols, no logos, no trademarks, no QR code, no price, no badge, no watermark. Keep upper-left copy zone truly clean and low detail.
